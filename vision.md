# MainStay (@mainstay) — concept re-envisioning

This note captures the concept change made in this thread. `@mainstay` stays;
YouTube/Spotify account integrations and listening-activity ingestion are
suggested future work, not implemented features.

## Concept
MainStay (@mainstay) is an entertainment and culture curation channel. It is an editorial identity and publishing platform, not a music channel with a manager attached. Music and artist promotion remain part of its programming, while the channel can also curate other stories, media, and moments that fit its South African point of view.

## Editorial principles
- The channel is the brand; artists and sources are programming, not its identity.
- Curate with context: explain why a track, story, or moment matters rather than reposting it without a point of view.
- Keep the voice street-authentic, energetic, direct, and non-corporate.
- One voice across TikTok, Instagram, and WhatsApp, adapted to each format.
- Keep the existing weekly Top-3 as a music-specific editorial feature within a broader channel concept; no need to abandon the ritual to broaden the brand.
- Do not imply that external account or listening-history integrations exist until they are implemented and authorized.

## Current foundation
The existing roster (Delah Dube and GOLIDE), artist media folders, SQLite schema, posting workflow, and platform handles remain intact. They are the initial programming inventory and operating system, not a limit on what MainStay can cover.
