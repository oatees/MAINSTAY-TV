## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

"DONT STOP" is a 32-second YouTube Short by PatricKxxLee that works as a branded performance clip for the track "Don't Stop" (tagged #doaa2). The whole video is shot in one location — a narrow alley with grey brick, a red metal door and a diamond-plate landing — and the artist performs straight to camera: shirtless, black Monster Energy beanie and neon-green Monster gloves, grillz, black jeans. The audio is a single repeated hook ("Don't stop") for the first 16 seconds, then a short flex verse (gold, Motorola, iPhone, "ride home"). Visually it is cut hard: scene-change detection flags roughly 31 cuts in 32 seconds, with bursts of 0.3-0.5 second shots around 6.8s, 8.3-8.9s, 14.6s and 17-19s. There is no on-screen text and no spoken CTA; the branding, the physique and the cut rhythm do the work. For MainStay TV, the lesson is packaging: one location, one visual signature, a repeated hook, and edit-to-the-beat density rather than new information.

## KEY TAKEAWAYS & ACTION PLAN

1. Hook-first, no preamble: open on the phrase "Don't stop" with the beat already running; no intro cards.
2. Drill the hook 3-4 times in the first 8 seconds — repetition is the retention engine, not new information.
3. Use one location and one visual signature: the alley + Monster beanie/gloves makes every frame instantly identifiable.
4. Cut to the beat: ~31 cuts in 32 seconds, with 1-2 second shots in the hook and 0.3-0.5 second bursts on the most quotable lines.
5. Put the verse on a physical action (push-up, flex, pose change) so the body carries the bar instead of a text overlay.
6. Keep verse bars to 3-5 words; pull the most quotable phrase ("going psycho") for on-screen text and the caption.
7. End on the strongest bar and a clean hero pose; let the loop act as the CTA and put the streaming link in the caption/pinned comment.
8. Brand integration: the Monster beanie/gloves are worn, not pasted on — the product is part of the performance, not a logo overlay.
