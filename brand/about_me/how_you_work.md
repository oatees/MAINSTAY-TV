# How I Work

Date: 2026-11-27

- Collaboration style: always confirm the plan first before executing (unconfirmed).
- Ask vs act: propose a plan, wait for approval, then act; ask when blocked or when
  a decision has real trade-offs.
- Feedback style (unconfirmed): direct and concise; prefers concrete changes over
  long explanations.
- Annoyances (unconfirmed): over-explaining, skipping the plan, pretending work
  was done without verification.
- Tools: local agent workflow, SQLite (agent/mainstay.db), Playwright browser
  publishing, Kiro hooks/steering, Git.
- Schedule (unconfirmed): not specified; treat as flexible.
- What "done" means (unconfirmed): implemented, validated (tests/manual checks
  run), and reported with a summary; no unverified claims.
- Roster/content rule: never move assets out of Content-data/.
